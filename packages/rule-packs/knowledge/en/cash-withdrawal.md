title: Why does a cash withdrawal on a credit card cost more?
keywords: cash withdrawal, cash advance, atm, credit card cash, fee
---

A cash withdrawal on a credit card has a fee, and charges apply from the day you withdraw, with no grace period. Purchases, by contrast, have no charges if you pay the full statement balance by the due date.

Withdrawing with a debit card from your current account avoids these charges.
