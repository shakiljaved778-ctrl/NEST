---
id: ibra
title: What is ibra when I settle a murabaha early?
keywords: ibra, rebate, murabaha, early settlement, settle early, profit
---

Ibra is a rebate the bank gives on the deferred profit that is not yet due when you settle a murabaha early. The bank's policy sets the ibra rate, and it can increase as more instalments are paid.

Because of this, the total you pay can be lower if you settle on a date after a rate change. AMIL checks each day in the coming weeks and shows the cheapest date.
