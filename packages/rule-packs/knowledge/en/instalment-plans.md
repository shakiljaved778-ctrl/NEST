title: How do card instalment plans work?
keywords: instalment plan, installment, epp, convert purchase, easy payment
---

An instalment plan spreads a card purchase over several months. Plans usually have a processing fee, and closing a plan early can have a fee too. If you close the card, active plans become payable in full.

Paying the purchase in full by the due date has no charge.
