---
id: annual-fee-refund
title: Do I get part of the annual fee back if I close my card?
keywords: annual fee, refund, close card, cancel card, pro rata
---

If the card's terms allow it, part of the annual fee is refunded when you close the card, in proportion to the full months left in the card year.

The exact refund for your card is shown before you confirm the closure.
