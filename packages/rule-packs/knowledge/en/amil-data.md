---
id: amil-data
title: What data does AMIL use?
keywords: amil, data, privacy, consent, ai, personal data
---

With your consent, AMIL reads your own Doha Demo Bank products and the bank's published rules to work out what you gain or lose. Every figure is calculated, not written by AI. If AI helps with the wording, it sees only the figures, never your name or account numbers.

You can turn AMIL on or off in Settings at any time.
