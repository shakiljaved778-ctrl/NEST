---
id: dormancy
title: What makes an account dormant?
keywords: dormant, dormancy, inactive, inactivity, reactivate, unused account
---

An account becomes dormant when you have made no transactions on it for the bank's dormancy period. A dormant account is restricted until you reactivate it, but the balance stays yours.

Any transaction you make, such as a transfer, deposit or card payment from the account, keeps it active. To reactivate a dormant account, contact the bank.
