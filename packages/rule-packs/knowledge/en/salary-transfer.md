title: What is linked to my salary transfer?
keywords: salary, salary transfer, move salary, salary account, waiver, salary linked
---

Some pricing and fee waivers depend on your salary being paid into the bank. Moving your salary elsewhere can change the instalments on salary-linked finance, and those waivers can end.

AMIL shows exactly what is linked to your salary before you confirm a change.
